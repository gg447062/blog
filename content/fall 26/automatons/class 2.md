for my mechanism i decided to make a cam. i was interested in them before my group got them as the topic for our presentation, and after doing our research i was doubly interested.

i first made a sketch of the basic idea i had for the whole mechanism, box included.
![[class 2-1790048011569.webp|395]]
then i grabbed some cardboard and dowels that i found in the shop. i cut the dowels down to size and cut out all the planes from cardboard and glued everything together with hot glue.

the first attempt was full of failures. i first tried to use a snail cam and a pear shaped cam but i made the pressure angles too steep so the followers kept getting stuck. i also had a very small diameter cam follower which made it almost impossible to keep it perched on the cam. i also had trouble with the hole in the top of the box through which the cam follower passed. once i made it big enough for the follower to pass through unobstructed the follower kept flopping over, despite adding another layer of carboard. i gave up and went home.

![[class 2-1790048027714.webp|342x257]]![[class 2-1790048049592.webp|342x257]]


later i perused pinterest for research and inspiration. i noticed that all the cam related mechanisms had used a very wide cam follower base, some sort of tube to constrain the follower shaft's movement, and more gently shaped cams. 

for round two i made all of the adjustments i had seen. i got a straw to house the cam follower, added a disc to the bottom, and switched to circular eccentric cams. right away, the mechanism worked much better and i was quite satisfied with the results.
