i want my project to be something i'm proud of, and something that is part of an idea or an exploration or a curiosity that extends beyond may of 2027. i'm afraid of it being something that is lame or uninspired, or that is something that i am just doing for the purpose of completing the project for school. i don't want it to be something that i just phone in because i'm burned out or afraid of committing to the idea fully. i want it to be something that represents my ethics and practice as well as my creative and aesthetic expression.
i care about the world, i care about everything, i care about too much. but if i had to focus on a few things, i care about being interconnected in a healthy and positive way, sharing the knowledge that i have so that others can benefit from it. i care about being able to make music that i'm proud of and that other people can enjoy. i care about using what i have and valuing what i have and not desiring more more more constantly and unendingly. i care about agency and equitable access to resources and freedom from persecution and fear. 
i'm dreaming of having a collection of synths and sound circuits that i have built and use to perform live all over the world, with a blog or online archive available to peruse so that other people are able to learn from the projects and make them themselves. i'm dreaming of having a job teaching things related to music and sound and circuits, but also balancing that with maintaining my practice as best as i can.
