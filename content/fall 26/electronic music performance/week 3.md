
**nicolas collins - microstoriasnd processed**

crazy textures, phasing, stereo movement, bits of songs peeking through the cloud of drones, percussive rhythmic parts, dynamics, hazy.

the music itself is interesting to me due to the above qualities which i like listening to, but i think it becomes far more interesting when i learned about the modded cd players used to make the piece. would be a great piece to see performed live


**Terry Riley - Music for the Gift III**

feels very modern, strange but not at all unpleasant mix of jazz and electronics. the delay adds interesting rhythms and dissonances. another one that would benefit from the performance context. could very well just be a dude with a sampler and a delay playing back pieces of recordings, but was recorded live with full instrumentation


**Yasunao Tone - Part II (1/3)**

way harsher than the collins piece, more obviously 'glitchy', sharper repetitions that sounded more like a broken machine, more unpredictable sharp jumps between sounds, timbres, rhythms, dynamics. a lot of space, silence. the collins piece felt soporific and almost relaxing, but this one was more stressful and raw.
