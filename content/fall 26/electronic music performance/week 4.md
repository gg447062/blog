~6 tracks of speed merengue by Atom

this is a crazy style of music, definitely falls on the dense side of the scale in our dense/sparse dichotomy from last week. extremely frenetic dance music that would go really hard at a party or club. very sped up and electrified version of merengue which is traditionally played with mostly acoustic instruments (although many modern merengue bands have incorporated electronic instruments in some form or another). there are some obviously electronically generated sounds but also some voices and brass instruments. my intuition is that all the sounds are either synthesized or sampled. they are all definitely manipulated electronically in any case.

a couple tracks of the cumbias rebajadas playlist 

this is the complete opposite of the speed spectrum, super slowed down and hauntingly beautiful dance music. one of my favorite kinds of music by far. i know from experience that this also goes extremely hard on the dance floor, albeit in a different way. cumbia rebajada is a great of example of a modern style of music wholly dependent on technology: this whole genre is really all about the playback. the songs themselves exist separately in their own right at the original playback speed, but what makes a rebajada is the slowing down of the record or tape or wav file etc. so really the rebajada doesn't exist in the actual performance and recording of the original song itself. it only exists through the misuse of the playback technology for artistic effect. 

a couple tracks by Ahmed Essyad

really beautiful dreamlike sounds in the beginning of the first track. high shiny chime sounds and electric chirps floating on a bed of dissonant echos. then goes into more chirpy and frenetic sounds with different attack and release times. it's giving bebe and louis barron's 'forbidden planet' score. this one has a nice range between dense and sparse sound. also nice blend of harsher and softer sounds
