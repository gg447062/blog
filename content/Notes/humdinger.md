
As a side project, I've been thinking about making an instrument inspired by the hurdy gurdy. AS of now it's pretty much a drone instrument, but it woud be cool to add some pitch control as well. The most basic version of it is two guitar strings vibrated by a rosin coated spinning disk heard by a pickup and amplified. I have wood, the motor, two guitar tuning knobs, and a pickup. I just need the strings.

here's a couple videos about hurdy gurdys:
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SyBYRlB6VW4
https://www.youtube.com/shorts/9mjx-jrkdak

I would like to avoid using an arduino for the motor control, but would also like to perhaps be able to vary the speed of the motor. I came upon a potential solution: a 555 pwm circuit controlling an h-bridge which drives the motor. The required circuits are outlined in the following links:

https://www.instructables.com/H-Bridge-on-a-Breadboard/

https://www.electronicdesign.com/technologies/embedded/article/21237625/electronic-design-a-555-timer-pwm-circuit-to-control-ancient-evil


**dimensions**

guitar neck length: 24-25.5 "
short scale bass: 30-32 "

want it to be amplified, could maybe have two speakers connected with long speaker wire so as to fill the room better

maybe add a built-in speaker to it a la this guitar
