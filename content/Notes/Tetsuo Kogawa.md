Tetsuo Kogawa is a multimedia artist working with radio. His work has an inspiring ethos and aesthetic vision of art and media. anarchic noise radio, micro radio circuits allow for lower barrier to entry into broadcasting, geographical intimacy of micro broadcasts allows for increased audience participation, micro means diversity.

all the information here is from his amazing [website](https://translocal.jp/index.html)

read his micro radio [manifesto](https://translocal.jp/radio/micro/index.html) for more information

***

*"As a critic of radio media, a broadcaster of free radio, a workshop leader of making one's own DIY transmitter, a public artist using transmitter, and a radioart performer, I have been trying to deconstruct transmitter and create something new not only in the social but also what 'art' should be per se"*

***

*"technology does not have to be only “high tech” but could also work on the scale of hand work and at the distance of human limbs*"

* * * * *

*"micro means diverse, multiple, and polymorphous"* 

***

*"Today, our microscopic space is under technologically control and surveillance. Our potentially diverse, multiple and polymorphous space is almost homogenized into a mass. Therefore we need permanent effort to deconstruct this situation."*
