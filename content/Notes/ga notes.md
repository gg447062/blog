I usually assumed that a “good” edit meant one that was smooth and almost invisible. But Murch made me realize that sometimes breaking continuity can actually make a scene stronger if the cut feels right emotionally. (jayda)

Editing too fast may leave the emotions unexpressed, while editing too slowly may make people feel bored. So editing is somewhat like music, and it needs to find the right rhythm. (jessica)

A cut in a film often becomes the blink and organizes emotion and thought for viewers. (ally)
